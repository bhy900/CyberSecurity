## X) Why would you want to use a feed reader (and RSS/Atom)?

If you regularly follow more than a few websites, a feed reader is usually more efficient than manually browsing each site. RSS/Atom provides a centralized, ad-light, structured view of new content, but it requires some setup and not every website supports it. For students, researchers, cybersecurity professionals, and news enthusiasts, the positives often outweigh the negatives.


## Y) Case Osuuspankki

- The case involved a 17 year old who used a port-scanning program to look for security weaknesses and attempt unauthorized access to a computer system. The Finnish Supreme Court ruled that this was a hacking or an intrusion attempt.

- The argument that someone else could have used his IP address was not enough to overturn the evidence. Software capable of carrying out the act was found on his computer.

- The Finnish Supreme Court considered the offense serious and did not reduce the damages, even though the offender was a minor. Protecting computer systems was considered important.
