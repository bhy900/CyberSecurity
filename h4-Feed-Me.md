## X) Why would you want to use a feed reader (and RSS/Atom)?

If you regularly follow more than a few websites, a feed reader is usually more efficient than manually browsing each site. RSS/Atom provides a centralized, ad-light, structured view of new content, but it requires some setup and not every website supports it. For students, researchers, cybersecurity professionals, and news enthusiasts, the positives often outweigh the negatives.
