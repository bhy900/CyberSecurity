## *As the school year begins, it is a good time to remind children about safe internet use*

- children get their first phone when they start school, so it is important to teach them safe internet habits from the beginning. Parents and guardians play a big role in this
- parents should learn about app age limits, privacy settings, and online risks because children can be exposed to harmful content, scams, or harassment online
- talking openly with children about their online experiences is more effective than relying only on technical controls and security settings
- children should learn how to recognize scams, fake information, manipulated images, and misleading online content. Media literacy is an important online safety skill
- when using AI tools, children should never share personal information, passwords, contact details, or other sensitive data
- basic device security is important: use strong passwords, do not share login details, enable location and remote-lock features, and know what to do if a device is lost or stolen
- children can practice online safety skills in Cyber City Tycoon, a game developed by Aalto University
